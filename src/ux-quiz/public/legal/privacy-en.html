Privacy Policy

1. Who processes the data

[Owner name or status] (“we”), owner of the mini app and the @ux_quiz_bot bot (the “Game”).

2. What data we receive

  • From Telegram when you open the Game: account ID, first and last name, profile photo link, interface language. Telegram passes this data to the mini app with a digital signature.
  • About the game: who invited you and whom you invited, how many questions of the first block you completed, your number of lives and when the next one regenerates (for the notification), and whether the notification is on.
  • About purchases: what you bought, for how many Stars, the status and the Telegram payment ID. We do not receive card details.
  • Support requests: the message you send after the /paysupport command.

The rest of your progress (answers, stats, settings) is stored in Telegram cloud storage and is not sent to us.

3. Why

  • to credit purchases and friend rewards, and never twice;
  • to show you your invited friends and their progress, and to show your name and photo in your friends’ lists;
  • to send a notification when a life regenerates, if you turned it on;
  • to answer support requests and resolve payment issues.

4. Who we share it with

We do not sell data. Data is stored on a server at [hosting provider, country]. Payments and messages go through Telegram under its privacy policy.

5. How long we keep it

While you use the Game. Purchase records — [period, e.g. 3 years] to resolve payment disputes. Database backups are deleted after 14 days.

6. Your rights

You can ask what data we hold about you, have it corrected or deleted — send /paysupport to the bot. You can turn off notifications in the Game settings or by blocking the bot.

7. Changes

We may update this policy. A new version takes effect when published on this page.
